# Drafts — free scratch workspace

This folder is the working area for **not-fully-committed files** in the portable architecture. Use it as a placeholder / staging space while an idea is still forming.

## Rules

- Content here is **not "live"** until promoted into `Concepts/`, `Methodology/`, `Process/`, `Skills/`, or `Templates/` via `_meta/REVIEW_QUEUE.md`.
- Agents may freely create, edit, and delete anything in `Drafts/` without the approval gate (the free lane).
- Do not link to `Drafts/` notes from live controlling docs; promote first, then link.
- Keep personal or work-specific residue out of here — that belongs in your `MY_MIND_ROOT` / `WORK_MIND_ROOT`, not this public edition.

See [[_meta/Vault-Conventions]] and [[Process/Approval-Workflow]] for the promotion flow.
