# Source: 0xMorlex FDE Roadmap (31 Jul 2026)

High-signal 8-step roadmap on the Forward Deployed Engineer / Applied AI Engineer role.

**Core claim:** 95% of enterprise AI projects fail on integration, not model capability. The high-paid role is the engineer who closes that gap.

**Key points captured:**

- Role definition and mental model (founding engineer on someone else’s product)
- Why the role exploded (AI broke generic SaaS + labs need speed + modern tooling economics)
- Market map (FDE / Applied AI Engineer / FDSE / Solutions Engineer etc.) and compensation bands
- Breadth over depth + product judgment + ROI framing
- Ship AI, do not train it — focus on evals and failure modes
- The three artifacts: MCP servers, agent skills, sub-agents
- Ship one real deployment for a real user and stay to fix what breaks
- Interview structure, especially the discovery round that eliminates strong coders
