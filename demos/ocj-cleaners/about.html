About

Your Mount Pleasant cleaner

OCJ Cleaners is a neighborhood dry cleaning and laundry shop on West Alabama Street — the kind of place where the counter knows how you like your jeans finished.

You’ll find us at 550 W Alabama St in Mount Pleasant, with a clear storefront sign and parking out front. We’re listed with the Mount Pleasant–Titus County Chamber under Cleaning Services and Dry Cleaning & Laundry Services.

Public Google feedback centers on three things: a sharp press and starch, friendly respectful help at the counter, and turnaround that keeps pace with a working week. That’s the reputation this demo site is built to carry — and to make reachable without a Facebook login wall.

Owner story, staff names, and history aren’t published on first-party pages we could read for this demo. When you’re ready to launch for real, a short paragraph from the shop turns this page from “good local proof” into “our story.”
